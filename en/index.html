your questions

Is MacGuard really free?

Yes, completely free: no limited trial, no subscription, no ads and no trackers.

Does the app connect to the internet?

Only to be useful: checking for MacGuard updates on GitHub, looking up your apps’ versions on the App Store and with their publishers, and, when you ask, testing your speed or showing your public IP address. No analytics tool is built in.

Is it safe for my files?

Nothing is removed without your confirmation. By default files go through the Trash, and the last cleanup can be undone. Two exceptions are erased for good: the contents of the Trash, if you tick it, and browsing traces. Check the list before you confirm.

Why does macOS block the first launch?

MacGuard is not notarized by Apple yet. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, then choose Open Anyway. You only need to do this once.

Which Macs does it run on?

Apple silicon Macs (M1 and later) with macOS 14 or later. Intel Macs are not supported.

What language is the app in?

French for now. This page is in English so you can see what it does.

Does it replace an antivirus?

It complements macOS protections: it checks your files against the signatures Apple publishes and spots adware and suspicious items. It is not real-time protection.

How do I uninstall it?

If you turned on the no-password option, turn it off in the settings first. Then drag MacGuard from Applications to the Trash.

install macguard

  1. Open the DMG and drag MacGuard into Applications.
  2. On first launch, open System Settings › Privacy & Security, then choose Open Anyway.
  3. After that, MacGuard updates in one click.
